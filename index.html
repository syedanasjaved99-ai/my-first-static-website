How do private viewings work?
Viewings are scheduled directly with a lead advisor. We confirm identity and liquidity context in advance, then coordinate access with sellers or estate managers—often after hours or via separate entrance protocols.
Do you represent both buyers and sellers?
Yes, on a selective basis. Where a conflict could arise, we disclose it early and align with independent counsel. Many of our mandates are exclusive or off-market on one side only.
What geographies do you cover?
We maintain active corridors across the Americas, UK & Europe, and select Middle Eastern markets. Engagements outside those regions are handled through vetted partner firms under Aurelia oversight.
How are fees structured?
Advisory fees reflect scope—acquisition, disposition, or long-term stewardship—and are agreed in writing before diligence begins. We do not monetize third-party referrals without disclosure.
